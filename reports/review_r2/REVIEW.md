## Decision

**Do not freeze S1 or S2 at this revision.** Their scientific development evidence is substantially improved and remains useful. The remaining blockers concern execution integrity, outcome completeness and one erroneous S1 resource summary. They need a bounded repair, not another large development campaign. S3 remains exploratory and is not proposed for confirmation. No production freeze record or confirmatory run was created by this review.

The revised S2 primary is accepted in substance: index-selected prediction minus exact compatible-set majority, equal weight across the five structured families, B16, 16 intervention queries per world. Keep 1,000 structured plus 1,000 IID uniform-H null worlds in the proposal; retain the existing endpoint, tie rule, interval and fixed allocation. No further search for a favorable primary is warranted. The observed +0.0009 interval crossing zero means no clear advantage detected, not equivalence and not a general refutation of simplicity priors.

Keep the misspecified population **as a descriptive model-class adequacy diagnostic**. Report empty-version-space rate and prediction coverage; zero-versus-zero scores when both selectors abstain are not comparative predictive evidence. Do not redesign this population after seeing its near-total collapse to manufacture useful-looking rankings. Keep structured-minus-null as a named exploratory difference, not another confirmatory primary. Preserve the distinction between the structured population conditioned on avoiding development isomorphisms and the unconditioned IID null.

The broader project still has a defensible contribution: compact symbolic representations support exact model-relative querying; the SAT hybrid extends coverage; and controlled induction experiments distinguish a coding prior from the benefit of aggregating compatible models. These results do not establish general real-world truth, an ordering of Kolmogorov complexities, or durable model learning.

## Findings requiring S1/S2 repairs

### R2-01 — concurrent resumes can overwrite a completed attempt (high; shared runner)

`src/causalguard/runner.py:255` opens/resumes without exclusive ownership. At lines 334–342 each process chooses its next attempt number from its own initial state and writes through `os.replace` (line 140). In a temporary fixture two simultaneous resumes both exited successfully, both logged successful attempt 1, and only one attempt file survived. Atomic replacement is not immutable publication.

Require exclusive execution ownership before recovery or work begins, refusal of an active second executor, and no-clobber publication of terminal attempts. A second process must not quarantine the first process's live temporary files. Test overlap deterministically and verify the original successful bytes remain intact. Evidence: [repro_concurrent.py](repro_concurrent.py), [runtime_evidence.json](runtime_evidence.json).

### R2-02 — hard supervisor death leaves worker computation alive (high; shared runner)

Workers call `setsid()` at `runner.py:444`. Consequently, killing the runner's process group does not kill those independent groups. Cleanup in the living supervisor does not run after SIGKILL. A fixture using the repository's `hung_with_child` left both worker and child alive after the supervisor group was killed. The review explicitly cleaned them up and verified they were gone.

Implement and test parent-death ownership/cleanup across supported platforms, including descendants and restart races. Resume must not start overlapping work while an old owner remains active. This is distinct from the now-working live-supervisor deadline test. Evidence: [repro_parent_death.py](repro_parent_death.py).

### R2-03 — unresolved failures become a successful completion on resume (medium; shared runner)

At `runner.py:354–359`, completion depends on this invocation's failure counter. A failed one-item run initially reports `complete_with_failures`; unchanged resume reports `complete` with one unresolved failure still present. The confirmatory CLI consequently returns success incorrectly.

Compute run-level terminal status from all latest attempts; retain invocation counters separately. Distinguish execution ending from study-specific scientific completeness. Preserve S1 algorithmic timeouts as outcomes. Do not add opportunistic retries or regenerate successful results to obtain completion.

### R2-04 — resume and analysis do not fully bind stored identities (medium; shared runner)

At `runner.py:268–273`, resume compares the stored digest string without recomputing the stored plan. A changed stored payload retaining its old digest is accepted by resume, although the read-only loader rejects it later. At lines 368–428, attempt validation accepts wrong `run_id`, `plan_sha256` and `source_tree_sha256`; only the latest payload hash is compared.

Validate the entire plan and every attempt, including superseded failures, before execution and analysis. Compare actual content identities, allowed status/schema, numbering and supersession; refuse inconsistency before recovery mutates scientific storage. Evidence for R2-03/04: [repro_runtime.py](repro_runtime.py).

### R2-05 — terminal success is mistaken for endpoint completeness (high for S2 analysis)

`analysis/s2_analysis.py:583–608` counts nominally successful worlds without checking that each contributes the prescribed primary. `world_scores` at lines 431–437 averages whichever queries remain. In temporary five-world fixtures, deleting one B16 cell produced `complete=true` with only four world contributions; deleting 15 of one world's 16 intervention queries produced `complete=true` with only 65 of 80 planned queries.

Validate endpoint-bearing cells, identities and exact query cardinalities against each planned world before scoring. Missing or duplicated scientific outcomes must be rejected or explicitly incomplete, never silently averaged. Report structured-primary completion separately from required-null and whole-study completion. A structured estimate can remain interpretable while its null panel is unfinished, but that is not a completed S2 study. Validate S1 query completeness under the same principle.

**This does not invalidate the published development primary:** its saved grids passed the additional completeness audit. Evidence: [repro_s2_contract.py](repro_s2_contract.py), [s2_contract_evidence.txt](s2_contract_evidence.txt).

### R2-06 — S1 memory summaries are false zeros (medium; published analysis)

`analysis/s1_analysis.py:74` receives scientific output without the attempt envelope's `worker_peak_rss_bytes`; lines 207–210 replace missing values by zero. All **49** published `peak_rss_mib_median` entries are zero. Raw examples instead contain 98,844,672 bytes for codecs, 33,783,808 for index_sat and 35,667,968 for literal.

Propagate validated resource metadata, use explicit missing values rather than measured zeros, and regenerate a versioned corrected summary. The raw measurements already exist; no S1 rerun is necessary for this correction. Correctness/completion counts are unaffected.

## S3 findings — offline correction, no new model calls needed

### R2-07 — target labels have a budget shortcut

At `s3_tasks.py:266` and 280, both budget and requested target verdict use `index % 3`. The generator establishes B3→entailed, B5→refuted, B8→underdetermined. This design-known, unfitted rule scores **80/100** on held-out target items and 18/20 in each diagnostic panel. The reported pooled surface baseline scores 47/100 on held-out targets and misses this role-specific shortcut (`s3_analysis.py:179–185`).

Disclose and score this baseline on the saved items. Any future S3 generator must independently randomize/counterbalance target verdict against public design features and report realized feasibility shortfalls. This weakens the target task's construct validity; it does not mathematically change the paired −0.08 estimate. Do not delete unfavorable items retrospectively.

### R2-08 — arm C's zero coherence contradictions is an aggregation-reporting bug

`llm/harness.py:105–136` combines a majority verdict with averaged probability vectors without recomputing `verdict_prob_mismatch`. The held-out balanced aggregates contain **134 argmax disagreements among 297 valid aggregates**, despite the reported 0/300. Use the same explicit tie/coherence policy after aggregation, with valid-vector denominators. Distinguish an aggregate disagreement from a directly stated model contradiction. Accuracy and the target contrast are unaffected.

### R2-09 — one supposedly fresh target repeats its source claim

At `s3_tasks.py:290–306`, syntactic novelty does not normalize initial edits. Held-out world index 2 has identical source/target claims: state (0,1,1,1), horizon 2, target x0=1; the target additionally edits x1 to its existing value 1. Report this case and an optional explicitly exploratory exclusion sensitivity while retaining the original focus estimate. Future novelty checks must canonicalize effective initial states and persistent interventions and label complements separately.

Evidence for R2-07–09: [repro_s3.py](repro_s3.py), [s3_evidence.json](s3_evidence.json).

## Reporting corrections and interpretation

- The sizing report's 0.021–0.032 false-positive range is **per tail**. Summing the two recorded tails gives a two-sided rate of **0.046–0.0545**, consistent with nominal 0.05.
- Under the uniform-H prior and exogenous evidence, majority **cannot be exceeded in expected accuracy** by another predictor. Equality is possible; the theorem does not require strict loss or predict −0.022 specifically.
- Gemma's floor stop is an acceptable disclosed development scope reduction. D29 was chosen after partial diagnostic inspection; it is not preregistration. Its 0.319 point estimate does not establish true below-chance ability or support two-model generalization from the expanded Qwen panel.
- Label balanced accuracy correctly: Qwen A's 0.433 is balanced accuracy; its ordinary accuracy on those 300 items is 125/300 = 0.417.
- S3 observation inputs are exogenous; oracle-balanced query selection is outcome-stratified. The current exogeneity test does not establish final-query independence.
- The certificate prompt includes verbal solver assurance as well as mechanisms. The observation supports retention under the **full certificate-plus-label prompt** during this critique, not an isolated proof of mechanistic comprehension. A future study could separate label, equal assurance and valid mechanistic material, crossed with critique. That is a new development design, not needed to repair this release.
- Brier values remain numerical scores of supplied vectors, not calibration measurements by themselves. Keep direct-response and aggregate coherence denominators distinct.
- Analysis labels and figure titles must derive development/fixture/confirmation phase from validated provenance. Current S2 success text and S1 figures hardcode development; future confirmation should not be mislabeled, nor should the reporting path silently re-size from final outcomes.
