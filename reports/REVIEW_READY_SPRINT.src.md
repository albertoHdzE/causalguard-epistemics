## 7. Where the director is wrong, or the report is still weak

1. **The title.** It is right to lead with the contrast. Two cautions. "Verify ... by lookup" is literally true on
   Tier W only; on Tier H the supplement shows Claude solving Task S (balanced accuracy
   {{R/supplement/balanced_accuracy_H}} on the finished worlds) and deferring once to a forged report it could not
   refute, so the title should not be read as a Tier H claim, and the abstract does not make one. And "frontier
   models" is two models, one at a non-default effort; "Two frontier models" would be more exact, at the cost of a
   longer title. I kept the director's title.
2. **P-A5 on the supplement.** D118 says to report P-A5 "if both halves are computable"; I computed the Claude half
   on all finished Tier H worlds ({{R/supplement/worlds_finished|int}}) because it is a Task S prediction. If the director reads the 12-per-cell gate
   as applying here, it becomes "not testable"; the table states the rule used.
3. **The supplement is selected by the timeout.** Seven worlds failed because Claude's first Tier H turn exceeded the
   locked 600 s; those are plausibly the harder worlds. The supplement can say what Claude did on the worlds it
   finished, not what it does on Tier H. Raising the timeout would have needed another amendment; I did not.
4. **The P2 "verdict and probability" finding is post-hoc** and rests on Claude's 24 P2 worlds; it is labelled so.
   Its local contrast (four models move their probabilities with the verdict) is descriptive, without intervals.
5. **Still one AI coder.** The second-coder sheet exists but has not been filled; the qualitative claims keep that
   weakness.
6. **Release identity.** The tag points at this commit; the asset and manifest are in the release; the manifest
   covers `report/report.pdf` as built in this commit, so any later rebuild must regenerate the manifest.
