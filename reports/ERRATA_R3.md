# Errata to the historical development releases (iteration 3)

The historical documents and records of `v0.1.0-dev` (content `371b04e`) and `v0.2.0-dev` (content `161123c`) are
left unchanged. Their summaries remain reproducible from those revisions (R2 analyses: `analyze-*-v2` at tag
`v0.2.0-dev`; v1: `scripts/reproduce_v1.sh`). This file lists every correction found by the Codex R2 review
(`reports/review_r2/`) or by iteration 3. Each entry gives the corrected statement and the R3 artifact that replaces
the historical one. No raw record was changed.
